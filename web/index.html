VA versus VV ECMO: conceptual comparison

VA • Venoarterial

Cardiopulmonary support

Venous blood is oxygenated extracorporeally and returned to the arterial circulation, providing circulatory as well as gas-exchange support.

Relevant considerations include native cardiac output, mixing physiology, distal perfusion, and arterial cannulation complications.
VV • Venovenous

Respiratory support

Venous blood is oxygenated extracorporeally and returned to the venous system. It primarily supports gas exchange rather than replacing circulatory function.

Relevant considerations include access/drainage, recirculation, native cardiac output, and membrane gas exchange.
